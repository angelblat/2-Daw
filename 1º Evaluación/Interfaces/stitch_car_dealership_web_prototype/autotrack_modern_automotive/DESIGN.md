## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premium, transparent, and confidence-driven marketplace for certified pre-owned and zero-kilometer automobiles. It reflects reliability, technological clarity, and automotive precision. By moving away from cluttered, high-friction legacy classifieds, it creates an atmosphere of frictionless decision-making and verifiable trust.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Minimalism**:
- Crisp white content cards resting over a calm, breathable neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`).
- High-contrast typography paired with deep, dignified forest emerald (`#265C4B`) anchoring buttons, primary brand elements, and key focal points.
- Fresh, subtle mint tints (`#EAF5F1`, `#D1EBE1`) for pill badges, category cards, and subtle interactive states.
- Generous breathing room, structural alignment, and whisper-thin borders that instill certified confidence and calm throughout the user's buying and selling journey.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high semantic trust, natural elegance, and crisp digital legibility:

- **Primary (`#265C4B`)**: Forest Emerald. Used for main action triggers, key headers, emphasized metrics, and primary navigational states.
- **Primary Hover (`#1E493B`)**: Darkened emerald for tactile hover response on buttons and interactive chips.
- **Secondary (`#3CB371`)**: Fresh Mint Leaf. Reserved for featured badges, active success indicators, and positive verification tags.
- **Tertiary / Tint (`#EAF5F1`)**: Soft Mint Mist. Serves as background fills for quick-action pills, subtle icon enclosures, and feature pill badges.
- **Neutral Base Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: A calm, slate-tinted off-white delivering soft contrast against bright vehicle photography.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white reserved for vehicle cards, input controls, filter pods, and modal surfaces.
- **Neutral Text Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep slate for maximum legibility on headlines and pricing labels.
- **Neutral Text Muted (`#64748B`)**: Neutral slate for technical specifications (mileage, transmission, fuel type).
- **Border / Hairline (`#E2E8F0`)**: Low-contrast boundary strokes maintaining crisp geometry without visual weight.
- **Footer Dark (`#1E293B`)**: Grounded dark slate delivering visual closure at the base of the page.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to unify technical automotive clarity with approachable modern styling.

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in weights 700 and 800 with tight line tracking to deliver strong visual presence, commanding buyer attention without feeling harsh.
- **Eyebrow Labels**: Uppercase styling with expanded letter spacing (+0.06em) in weight 700, serving as orientation guides above section headings (e.g., `COCHEs DE OCASIÓN Y KM 0`, `DESTACADOS`).
- **Data & Pricing**: Prices receive bold emphasis (`title-price`, 800 weight) to provide immediate scan value across card grids.
- **Specifications & Body**: Rendered in regular (400) and medium (500) weights, keeping secondary car attributes legible and uncrowded.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a maximum container boundary of `1280px`:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` outer canvas padding. Vehicle listings display as 4-column or 3-column modular cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters. Vehicle listings reflow to 2 columns, and filter bars collapse into horizontal sliders or an off-canvas drawer.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters, `1rem` outer canvas padding. Vehicle cards span full width (1 column layout) with horizontal swipeable pills for categories.

Spacing rules prioritize clarity: sections are separated by generous vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` / 64px), while intra-card details maintain dense, structured clarity with `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **ambient, micro-diffused depth** coupled with structural **low-contrast borders** to evoke cleanliness and technical precision:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`) with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Inputs)**: `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns)**: Elevated to `0 8px 24px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. The border shifts smoothly from `#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Search Pods & Dialogs)**: Floating filter bar and overlays use `0 16px 36px rgba(38, 92, 75, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Image Overlays**: Subtle gradient vignette (`rgba(0,0,0,0.2)` to transparent) across top corners of vehicle photos ensures favorite icons and badges stay legible.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances modern softness with structured balance:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard for inputs, category cards, and utility buttons.
- **Container Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Used for primary vehicle cards, hero imagery framing, and promotional CTA blocks.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Reserved for status tags (`Destacado`), category filter pills, icon badges, and circular favorite buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep emerald fill (`#265C4B`), pure white text, 8px radius, `12px 24px` padding. On hover: shifts to `#1E493B`. Focus state adds `3px` ring in `#D1EBE1`.
- **Secondary / Soft CTA**: Mint mist fill (`#EAF5F1`), emerald text (`#265C4B`), transparent border. Used for in-card actions like `"Ver detalle →"`. On hover: tint deepens to `#D1EBE1`.
- **Ghost / Favorite Button**: Round `36px × 36px` pure white circle floating top-right on car images with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` border and subtle backdrop blur.

### Vehicle Cards
- **Structure**: Rounded `16px` white container bounded by `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Media Header**: Aspect ratio `16:10` image, overflow hidden. Contains a floating pill badge (`"Destacado"`) in `#3CB371` or `#265C4B` on the bottom-left/top-left and a round favorite toggle top-right.
- **Content Area**: 
  - Vehicle Title (`headline-sm`, 600 weight) and trim subtitle (`body-sm`, `#64748B`).
  - Spec Grid: 2×2 layout displaying Year, Mileage, Fuel type, and Gearbox with muted micro-icons.
  - Price & CTA Bar: Prominent price (`20px`, 800 weight) aligned left, paired with a compact `"Ver detalle →"` soft button aligned right.

### Filter & Search Pod
- A unified floating panel with `12px` roundedness, white surface, and elevated shadow.
- Divided into segmented select triggers (Marca, Modelo, Precio máximo) separated by vertical hairlines (`#E2E8F0`), flanked by a direct emerald action button (`"Buscar"`).

### Category Chips & Tiles
- Compact white surface cards (`12px` radius) featuring centered automotive silhouette artwork, body-type label below, and subtle hover elevation with emerald text highlight.

### Trust & Guarantee Indicators
- Trust blocks featuring a soft mint circular container (`48px × 48px`, `#EAF5F1`) housing delicate outline icons, backed by a bold mini-heading and brief reassuring description.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Flat `#FFFFFF` fields with `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `8px` radius, and dark slate text. Active and focus states transition border to `#265C4B` without heavy glow.
